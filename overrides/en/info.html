What kind of game is this?

Capture My Heart! is a browser arcade game built around drawing capture lines, sealing off territory, and revealing stage artwork. Move from safe territory into an unclaimed area while holding CAPTURE, draw a line, and reconnect it to safety. Once the line closes, the side without the boss becomes yours.

Long lines can capture large areas quickly, but they remain exposed to the boss and projectiles for longer. Small cuts are safer; large cuts are faster. Most of the strategy comes from deciding when the extra risk is worth it.

CLEAR RULE

80% · 90% · 99%+

80% clears the stage. 90% unlocks the bonus service scene. 99%+ is the PERFECT target for players who want to push the map almost completely open.

Ten stages with rising pressure

The public beta contains ten themed stages: Maid Cafe, Racing Girl, Office Lady, Miko, Idol, Resort, Wizard, Gothic, Kung-fu, and Final Queen. Difficulty rises through boss speed, projectile frequency, minions, chase pressure, and additional mechanics.

PANIC and RAGE pressure can occur across the game. LASER behavior is added from Stage 3, TERRITORY BREAK from Stage 5, and WARP from Stage 8. Attacks are intended to be readable, but careless long captures become increasingly dangerous.

PC and mobile

PC controls support custom key mapping. On mobile, the game uses an on-screen joystick plus CAPTURE and DASH controls. Landscape mode is recommended for the widest board view.

Public beta development

Anonymous play data and player feedback are used to improve difficulty and usability. Experimental changes can be rolled back when they create progression-blocking regressions.

Complete Collection reward

Completing Stage 10 unlocks the Complete Collection request flow. If the reward screen is closed by mistake, MY COLLECTION can be reopened for 72 hours on the same browser. See the Privacy Policy for email and token handling details.

The game is intended for players aged 19 and over and contains non-explicit fan-service artwork.
