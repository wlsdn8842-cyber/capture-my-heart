Privacy · Public Beta

Privacy Policy

Capture My Heart! can be played without creating an account. The service processes the information needed for public-beta operation, game improvement and optional features.

Anonymous play analytics

The public beta may store an anonymous visitor ID and session ID and collect events such as game start, stage entry, clear, death, completion, and session duration. Related context can include game version, stage, score, capture percentage, lives, screen size, device type, browser language, and referring host.

Anonymous analytics do not require your name, email address, exact location, or an account.

Feedback

Feedback may include ratings, selected tags, optional comments, and play context. The normal feedback form does not ask for a name or email.

Complete Collection email

If you complete all stages and request a Collection link, an email address is collected for reward delivery. Reward-delivery addresses are configured for short retention, while the access link uses a random token rather than putting the email address in the URL.

Optional marketing consent

Marketing email information is stored only when the user actively selects the separate optional consent checkbox. Marketing consent is not required for the Collection reward.

Browser storage and advertising

Game progress, settings, key mapping, language choice, and some queued events may be stored in the browser. The service may use third-party advertising services such as Google AdSense; if advertising is enabled, cookies or similar technologies may be used under the provider's policies.
