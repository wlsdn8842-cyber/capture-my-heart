Support & Feedback

Bug reports and player feedback

Use anonymous in-game feedback for most play comments. Email is available for issues that need direct follow-up.

In-game feedback

The FEEDBACK button lets you send ratings, tags, and an optional comment without entering a name or email. Play context such as stage and game version may be included.

Email

For privacy questions, Collection reward problems, or bugs that need direct follow-up, email wlsdn8842@gmail.com.

Useful bug-report details

  • Stage and approximate capture percentage.
  • PC or mobile and browser name.
  • The action immediately before the issue.
  • Whether the same steps reproduce it.
  • A full-screen screenshot if possible.

Hide email addresses, tokens, account details, or other sensitive information before sending screenshots.
