Public Beta Changelog

What changed, and why?

These notes record both successful releases and experiments that were rolled back after testing.

v0.13.7 · Korean / English

2026-10-19

Added a title-screen language selector and English versions of the public information pages.

v0.13.6 · Content Hub

2026-10-19

Added crawlable Game Info, Play Guide, Updates, FAQ, Contact and Privacy pages, plus sitemap, robots.txt, ads.txt and structured metadata.

v0.13.5 · Key Mapping

2026-10-06

Added persistent PC key mapping with duplicate-key checks and default reset.

v0.13.4 · HUD Audio Controls

2026-10-05

Added in-game BGM toggle and volume controls.

Movement v2 experiment · Rolled back

2026-10-05

Boundary-following movement was promising, but repeated stranded-player regressions appeared after boss territory changes, so the experiment was rolled back to the stable baseline.

v0.13.1 · Collection Reliability

2026-10-05

Fixed a server-side database permission issue that caused Collection requests to fail after reaching the backend.

v0.13.0 · Difficulty v2

2026-10-05

Raised the overall difficulty curve, especially in later stages, while retaining readable boss telegraphs.
